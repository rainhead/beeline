# Volunteers identifying their own specimens

Part of Beeline's architecture notes; the index is [README.md](README.md).

`/determinations` (`src/app/determine.ts` + `src/app/views/determine.tsx`, the island `src/app/islands/determine/`, beeline-bcq) is where a volunteer says what their pinned specimens are: every specimen is already a row, so a number is typed only to *find* rows, never to enter a name; ticked rows are named all at once (a box is all *Bombus*, then three refined to species), and the Caste column is one click — gyne/worker/drone where `animal_castes` (`schema/119`) says the taxon has castes, female/male elsewhere (the museum databases' words, Peter 2026-10-31; a queen is a gyne). Which taxa have castes is stated, not derived, in `animal_caste` (`schema/045`), nearest stated ancestor winning: the tree has no subgenus to say that cuckoo bumble bees have no workers, and the expert records cannot either (24 *B. flavidus* "workers" on the dev store). Two views over one grid: **By season**, a season in collecting order, chosen from a menu (`sample_season`, `schema/102` — the calendar year of a sample's first day), and **By field number**, the batch: specimens gathered by field number in the order added, kept in `determination_batch` so a box can be worked over several sittings. Numbers are read by `src/determination-numbers.ts` — whole, a unique tail (`412`), or a range whose start may be a tail and whose end may be abbreviated (`9685-697`) — across **every** season, since a box sorted by genus mixes years and numbers before 2025 have seven digits; its problems are codes and the catalog says them, with the span of the person's numbers by length so a dropped digit shows. **Entry writes drafts, never determinations** (Peter, 2026-10-01): `determination_draft` holds one changeable row per specimen and determiner, nothing downstream reads it, and the `commit-determinations` job (1am Pacific, `src/commit-determinations.ts`) turns each into an `in_app` determination in one transaction — before the 4am legacy export, so the export carries the day's work — taking only drafts changed before it began and dropping any that say what the newest volunteer determination already says. That is CONTEXT.md's draft/commit boundary, with one consumer and no per-export bookkeeping; the screen says it as what it means to the person — undo is free until 1am, and after that a change is kept beside the earlier name. A selection that has been named, or had its sex set, from the bar or in one of its rows' own cells, is finished with: the next row ticked or gone to starts a new selection, while unticking only narrows it (`src/app/islands/determine/selection.ts`). Before that a volunteer named one group, selected a second and named it, and the first group took the second name too, most easily through **Go to field number**, which selects the row it finds while the earlier rows are out of sight ([#316](https://github.com/rainhead/beeline/issues/316)). By season, that box stays at the top of the screen with the naming row. The name box takes only names from the list, so it turns off the computer's autocorrect and suggested words, which on a Mac replaced half a genus with an English word that matched nothing. Reach is the listings' `mine`, following the acting-for switch; the determiner — and owner of drafts and batch — is whoever is signed in, since delegation grants reach and never credit; impersonation looks and cannot write; someone else's specimen answers 404. Expert determinations are deliberately absent from the screen so a volunteer determines rather than copies (beeline-bcq's anti-anchoring note); the listings and the specimen page still show them. `db:reseed` carries none of it and says how much it left behind, like print runs. The design was chosen from three variants on a prototype branch, `prototype/volunteer-determinations`.
