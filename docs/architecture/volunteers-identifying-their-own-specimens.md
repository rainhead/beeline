# Volunteers identifying their own specimens

Part of Beeline's architecture notes; the index is in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#architecture-overview).

`/determinations` (`src/app/determine.ts` + `src/app/views/determine.tsx`, the island `src/app/islands/determine/`, beeline-bcq) is where a volunteer says what their pinned specimens are: every specimen is already a row, so a number is typed only to *find* rows, never to enter a name; ticked rows are named all at once (a box is all *Bombus*, then three refined to species), and the Caste column is one click — gyne/worker/drone where `animal_castes` (`schema/119`) says the taxon has castes, female/male elsewhere (the museum databases' words, Peter 2026-10-31; a queen is a gyne). Which taxa have castes is stated, not derived, in `animal_caste` (`schema/045`), nearest stated ancestor winning: the tree has no subgenus to say that cuckoo bumble bees have no workers, and the expert records cannot either (24 *B. flavidus* "workers" on the dev store). Two views over one grid: **by sample**, a season in collecting order (`sample_season`, `schema/102` — the calendar year of a sample's first day), and **the batch**, specimens gathered by field number in the order added, kept in `determination_batch` so a box can be worked over several sittings. Numbers are read by `src/determination-numbers.ts` — whole, a unique tail (`412`), or a range whose start may be a tail and whose end may be abbreviated (`9685-697`) — across **every** season, since a box sorted by genus mixes years and numbers before 2025 have seven digits; its problems are codes and the catalog says them, with the span of the person's numbers by length so a dropped digit shows. **Entry writes drafts, never determinations** (Peter, 2026-10-01): `determination_draft` holds one changeable row per specimen and determiner, nothing downstream reads it, and the `commit-determinations` job (1am Pacific, `src/commit-determinations.ts`) turns each into an `in_app` determination in one transaction — before the 4am legacy export, so the export carries the day's work — taking only drafts changed before it began and dropping any that say what the newest volunteer determination already says. That is CONTEXT.md's draft/commit boundary, with one consumer and no per-export bookkeeping; the screen says it as what it means to the person — undo is free until 1am, and after that a change is kept beside the earlier name. Reach is the listings' `mine`, following the acting-for switch; the determiner — and owner of drafts and batch — is whoever is signed in, since delegation grants reach and never credit; impersonation looks and cannot write; someone else's specimen answers 404. Expert determinations are deliberately absent from the screen so a volunteer determines rather than copies (beeline-bcq's anti-anchoring note); the listings and the specimen page still show them. `db:reseed` carries none of it and says how much it left behind, like print runs. The design was chosen from three variants on a prototype branch, `prototype/volunteer-determinations`.
