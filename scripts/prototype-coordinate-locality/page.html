PROTOTYPE: localities from coordinates

The question: can Beeline name a sample's locality from its coordinates well enough that volunteers stop having to fix iNaturalist's? A volunteer reads the locality to remember where they collected, or to find a specimen by its label, so it should name a town or peak they would know, in the same county as the point, and fit on the label (18 characters, no street address). A point inside a national wildlife refuge is named for the refuge. Change the settings and every number and row below is worked out again.

Throwaway (beeline-7i6). Towns and peaks come from GeoNames, counties from the 2023 Census boundaries, BC regional districts from the province's current boundaries (ABMS), refuges from USFWS. The rows are samples from the sandbox store, with obscured and private observations left out. This page holds sample coordinates, so keep it off the web.

How it does, by season

"Agrees" means the generated name and the sample's present locality contain each other once spelling is folded (Mount/Mt, punctuation). The present locality is mostly iNaturalist's place guess, accepted onto a label, so agreement is a rough check against what staff have printed, not a measure of correctness.
