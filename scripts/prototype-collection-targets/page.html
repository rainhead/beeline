Where the numbers come from. Specimen records in the old system as pulled on 24 October 2026 (396,877 records, 2017–2026), placed in counties and BC regional districts by their coordinates. A sample here is one collector, one day, one place, one host plant.
Species names are the taxonomists' determinations, which so far are mostly Oregon's; Washington, Idaho, BC and New Mexico have far fewer, and 2026's are largely not back yet. So "never recorded in Idaho" often means "never determined in Idaho".
"Mostly on" means at least 60% of a bee species' records with a host plant came from one plant genus, out of at least 8 records. It suggests a specialist but does not prove one.
