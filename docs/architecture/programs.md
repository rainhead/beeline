# Programs

Part of Beeline's architecture notes; the index is in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#architecture-overview).

`/programs` and `/programs/:code` (`src/app/programs.ts` + `src/app/views/programs.tsx`, beeline-rrit) gather what Beeline knows about each program, which was said in a dozen places: its mark, its leads, how many people belong to it, this season's collectors, samples and specimens, every season's records, whether it prints its own labels, the Ecdysis collection its expert determinations come back from (`ECDYSIS_COLLECTIONS` now names the program), and how to reach it. Licences and privacy policies stay on `/exports`, whose business they are. Read by everyone signed in, like the taxonomy, since it shows totals and the program's own leads and nobody's records; the links into the listings and the roster are drawn for staff only. A program is addressed by its code, never by `entity_id`. Its records follow the archives' rule — an atlas's ground, Master Melittology's outside every atlas, and none by geography for the BLM surveys. Contact is the leads, an atlas's website from `ATLAS_IDENTITY` beside its mark, and the email address the program gives for questions, which staff set on the page: each save is a line appended to `data/program-contacts.csv` (`src/program-contacts.ts`, `BEELINE_PROGRAM_CONTACTS`), the latest line for a program standing, so the file is its own history and the nightly backup carries it. Staff never edit a CSV. The address may be a staff member's work address, as the refuge project gives August Jackson's, but never one taken from the legacy register.
