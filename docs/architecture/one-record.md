# One record

Part of Beeline's architecture notes; the index is in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#architecture-overview).

`/samples/:id` and `/specimens/:id` (`src/app/record.ts` + `src/app/views/record.tsx`, beeline-2c3.34) are the two record pages, and the specimen one exists because `determination` is append-only and `determination_of_record` is a view: a correction is a newer event and an expert never overwrites a volunteer, and the specimen listing gave that whole history one cell. The page is the events — who, when, through which channel, expert or not, the qualifier, `verbatim_identification`, which row is the record — newest first and with the record *marked* rather than hoisted, because hoisting it rebuilds the flattened read this replaces. The record is frequently not the newest (61,102 specimens on the dev store), so where they differ the page says why. The grain split follows the data: QC findings are keyed to the sample, so "why will this not print" is answered on the sample's page, which also carries the coordinates with their `source` provenance and the observation's geoprivacy beside them, the elevation with its `elevation_source`, and a paged list of its specimens — the largest trap sample holds 2,252. Each page carries the whole of the other's sample block, through the same components, because a determination read without where and when the insect was collected is not a record of anything. Everyone reaches every record, and the location rule is the listings' (`withheld`): somebody else's protected sample says why it gives no location, in which of the two ways iNaturalist kept it — the collector's own choice, or a rare host plant. A record is addressed by `entity_id`, which a rebuild redraws; there is no better handle, since a field number is nullable and, across the four identifier eras, not unique.

The flags card on `/samples/:id` lists, after the flags themselves, every waiver on the sample with who waived it and why, to everyone who sees the flags — a waived flag is not among the flags, so this is where the collector learns why it stopped asking — and says when one no longer applies because the flag has changed. Its recorded flag text is withheld where the page withholds the location. For admins the card also carries a *Waive this flag* form under each waivable blocking flag and the hold form (`recordQcDecisions` in `src/app/record.ts`, beeline-plu, beeline-1kb.9).
