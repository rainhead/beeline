# Volunteers identifying their own specimens

Part of Beeline's architecture notes; the index is [README.md](README.md).

`/determinations` (`src/app/determine.ts` + `src/app/views/determine.tsx`, the island `src/app/islands/determine/`, beeline-bcq) is where a volunteer says what their pinned specimens are: every specimen is already a row, so a number is typed only to *find* rows, never to enter a name; ticked rows are named all at once (a box is all *Bombus*, then three refined to species), and the Caste column is one click — gyne/worker/drone where `animal_castes` (`schema/119`) says the taxon has castes, female/male elsewhere (the museum databases' words, Peter 2026-10-31; a queen is a gyne). Which taxa have castes is stated, not derived, in `animal_caste` (`schema/045`), nearest stated ancestor winning: the tree has no subgenus to say that cuckoo bumble bees have no workers, and the expert records cannot either (24 *B. flavidus* "workers" on the dev store). Two views over one grid: **By season**, a season in collecting order, chosen from a menu (`sample_season`, `schema/102` — the calendar year of a sample's first day), and **By field number**, the batch: specimens gathered by field number in the order added, kept in `determination_batch` so a box can be worked over several sittings. Numbers are read by `src/determination-numbers.ts` — whole, a unique tail (`412`), or a range whose start may be a tail and whose end may be abbreviated (`9685-697`) — across **every** season, since a box sorted by genus mixes years and numbers before 2025 have seven digits; its problems are codes and the catalog says them, with the span of the person's numbers by length so a dropped digit shows. **Entry writes drafts, never determinations** (Peter, 2026-10-01): `determination_draft` holds one changeable row per specimen and determiner, nothing downstream reads it, and the `commit-determinations` job (1am Pacific, `src/commit-determinations.ts`) turns each into an `in_app` determination in one transaction — before the 4am legacy export, so the export carries the day's work — taking only drafts changed before it began and dropping any that say what the newest volunteer determination already says. That is CONTEXT.md's draft/commit boundary, with one consumer and no per-export bookkeeping; the screen says it as what it means to the person — undo is free until 1am, and after that a change is kept beside the earlier name. A selection that has been named, or had its sex set, from the bar or in one of its rows' own cells, is finished with: the next row ticked or gone to starts a new selection, while unticking only narrows it (`src/app/islands/determine/selection.ts`). Before that a volunteer named one group, selected a second and named it, and the first group took the second name too, most easily through **Go to field number**, which selects the row it finds while the earlier rows are out of sight ([#316](https://github.com/rainhead/beeline/issues/316)). By season, that box stays at the top of the screen with the naming row. A number that is not in the year shown is looked for in every year (`locateSpecimen`, `GET /determinations/locate`), and the page opens that year with the specimen selected, at `/determinations?specimen=<id>`, an address a link to one specimen can use too (beeline-mjml). That replaced asking for an "All years" choice in the menu, which Karla Salp suggested on 2026-11-13 when finding her specimens of one bee across years meant switching the year for each: it would load every specimen a person has into one page, several thousand for her and 40,000 for the busiest collector (Peter, 2026-11-13). The screen calls a season a **collection year**, "By collection year" and "Collection year", since to a volunteer a season reads as spring or summer; the code and CONTEXT.md keep the word season. The name box takes only names from the list, so it turns off the computer's autocorrect and suggested words, which on a Mac replaced half a genus with an English word that matched nothing. Safari also offered the Mac's contacts in the box, whatever `autocomplete` said, so the box's `name` and `id` call it a search, the one thing that stopped that on a test page of variants (beeline-9u06). For someone working by keyboard or screen reader (beeline-tv0w): the page keeps what it scrolls to clear of the pinned bar, by measuring the bar and setting `scroll-padding-top`, since a browser otherwise brings a focused control only to the top edge of the window, under the bar; closing a row's name box puts focus back on that row's name button; a row's name button, name box and caste buttons each say the row's field number, so rows can be told apart by ear; and what an action did (`Named 5 Osmia.`, `Selected 26000130.`) is written where a screen reader announces it. Reach is the listings' `mine`, following the acting-for switch; the determiner — and owner of drafts and batch — is whoever is signed in, since delegation grants reach and never credit; impersonation looks and cannot write; someone else's specimen answers 404. Expert determinations are deliberately absent from the screen so a volunteer determines rather than copies (beeline-bcq's anti-anchoring note); the listings and the specimen page still show them. `db:reseed` carries none of it and says how much it left behind, like print runs. The design was chosen from three variants on a prototype branch, `prototype/volunteer-determinations`.
