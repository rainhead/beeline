# One record

Part of Beeline's architecture notes; the index is [README.md](README.md).

`/samples/:id` and `/specimens/:id` (`src/app/record.ts` + `src/app/views/record.tsx`, beeline-2c3.34) are the two record pages, and the specimen one exists because `determination` is append-only and `determination_of_record` is a view: a correction is a newer event and an expert never overwrites a volunteer, and the specimen listing gave that whole history one cell. The page is the events — who, when, through which channel, expert or not, the qualifier, `verbatim_identification`, which row is the record — newest first and with the record *marked* rather than hoisted, because hoisting it rebuilds the flattened read this replaces. The mark says "current", Ecdysis's word for it and the plainer one to a volunteer (Peter, 2026-11-06), and a row's cells line up on their first line. The record is frequently not the newest (61,102 specimens on the dev store), so where they differ the page says why. The grain split follows the data: QC findings are keyed to the sample, so "why will this not print" is answered on the sample's page, which also carries the coordinates with their `source` provenance and the observation's geoprivacy beside them, the elevation with its `elevation_source`, and a paged list of its specimens — the largest trap sample holds 2,252. The specimen page carries the sample's facts and where it was collected, through the same components, because a determination read without where and when the insect was collected is not a record of anything. The sample's flags it shows as the samples listing's chips after the sample's name (`FlagChips`), each linking to the sample page, which says the rest; it has a Flags card of its own only for a flag about the specimen itself (beeline-8w8n). Both pages name the **program** the sample was collected for, its atlas or else Master Melittology itself, linked to the program's page, and draw the program's mark at the top right where it has one ([Programs](programs.md)). The method is one row, the kind with the protocol beside it only where the protocol says more, since every sample made from an observation carries the protocol "aerial net". Everyone reaches every record, and the location rule is the listings' (`withheld`): somebody else's protected sample says why it gives no location, in which of the two ways iNaturalist kept it — the collector's own choice, or a rare host plant. A sample citing an observation iNaturalist no longer has (`observationGone` in `src/app/listings.ts`, beeline-wcc6) shows the id unlinked with the day it was first found missing, offers no button to iNaturalist and, since the edit form only corrects a sample that cites no observation, no edit either: its missing-upstream flag says staff will look into it. Its location stays withheld from other volunteers, because nobody can now say whether it was private, and the page says that rather than promising a read that will not come. A record is addressed by `entity_id`; there is no better handle, since a field number is nullable and, across the four identifier eras, not unique.

Where a staff inventory has said what became of the pin, the specimen page says so under the labels: the disposition where the remark states one (in the collection, or missing), the remark as the store holds it, and who stated it and when (`specimen_disposition`, beeline-fud5; [docs/research/2025-inventory.md](../research/2025-inventory.md) is the file it comes from, and `pnpm disposition:load` the loader). A specimen nobody has remarked on shows nothing, since for the ordinary pin there is nothing to say.

The flags card on `/samples/:id` lists, after the flags themselves, every waiver on the sample with who waived it and why, to everyone who sees the flags — a waived flag is not among the flags, so this is where the collector learns why it stopped asking — and says when one no longer applies because the flag has changed. Its recorded flag text is withheld where the page withholds the location. For admins the card also carries a *Waive this flag* form under each waivable blocking flag and the hold form (`recordQcDecisions` in `src/app/record.ts`, beeline-plu, beeline-1kb.9).

The sample page's Place row carries the samples listing's pencil (`PlaceEdit`, beeline-8w8n), offered by the same rule (`samplePlaceEdit` beside `placeEditable`): it opens, in the row, a combobox of iNaturalist's locality, the names Beeline suggests from the coordinates with why in a word or two ("nearby town"), and the current one, free for staff to write any name in, and posts to `/samples/:id/place` like the listing's ([Browsing](browsing-scope-filters-sort.md), [Localities from coordinates](localities-from-coordinates.md)). A collector chooses among the names offered, and takes back one they set by choosing iNaturalist's. Staff are offered it on a printed sample too, to correct the record before a reprint, with a line saying the labels keep the old name; the staff-only "Set the locality" form and its route are gone. Staff set coordinates the same way, from a pencil on the Coordinates row that opens the latitude, longitude and accuracy, with a second button that follows iNaturalist again where staff coordinates stand (`/samples/:id/coordinates`, beeline-942). Reprinting is per specimen (Peter, 2026-11-06): the sample page has no reprint form ([Printing](printing.md)).
