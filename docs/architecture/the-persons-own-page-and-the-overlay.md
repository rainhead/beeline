# The person's own page, and the overlay

Part of Beeline's architecture notes; the index is [README.md](README.md).

The person's own page is where the work is done and so explains itself at length; a store with no legacy staging drops the apparatus entirely rather than explaining its absence. Staff act there too — rebind or unbind an account, grant or revoke admin, set where someone belongs (`person_membership`), say who may act for whom (`person_delegate`), say which programs someone leads (`program_lead`, beeline-7c1: whose work a program's is, never access — each atlas is a `program`, beside it Master Melittology `MM` and the BLM surveys `BLM`, and the overlay field `leads` names the whole set by code, semicolon-separated like `acts_for`), fix name parts — and every one of those writes goes to `data/person-overlay.csv` first and is applied from there (`src/person-overlay.ts`, `src/apply-person-overlay.ts`), so promotion replays it onto a rebuilt store. `create` asserts that a person exists at all: promotion mints people from records, so a staffer who collects nothing — an intern, a coordinator — has no other way into the store and therefore no way to sign in, since the approval gate is an account bound to a person. It names them `name:<display name>`, is applied before every other row so the account binding and the admin grant compose with it, is idempotent on replay, and refuses a name two people already share rather than minting a third (beeline-2c3.32); a hand-written INSERT would do the same thing once and be gone at the next `db:reseed`. It has two screens. The unknown collectors' (below) admits somebody who has records. `/people/new` (`src/app/add-person.ts`, beeline-2c3.33) admits somebody who has none, by their iNaturalist account: staff type a login or a profile link, the account is looked up on iNaturalist's API, and the form writes `create`, the account, the name parts and, for staff, `admin`, through the same `commitDecisions` and refusals as an add from unknown collectors. The id is read from iNaturalist again when the form is posted rather than trusted from it, so the login written beside the id is the one iNaturalist has then. An account somebody already holds, or one with collection records waiting, is not offered the form: the page says who holds it, or links to unknown collectors, whose add also makes the records into samples. Below the form are the people turned away at sign-in: `private.inat_sign_in` is written before the approval gate asks whether anyone holds the account, so the record less the accounts somebody now holds is everyone who tried and was refused. Before this page, a staff member could only be added by a row in `ingest/person-overlay.csv` and a deploy, and on the first day after cutover one was turned away at sign-in until a pull request merged.
