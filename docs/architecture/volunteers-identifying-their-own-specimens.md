# Volunteers identifying their own specimens

Part of Beeline's architecture notes; the index is [README.md](README.md).

`/determinations` (`src/app/determine.ts` + `src/app/views/determine.tsx`, the island `src/app/islands/determine/`, beeline-bcq) is where a volunteer says what their pinned specimens are: every specimen is already a row, so a number is typed only to *find* rows, never to enter a name; ticked rows are named all at once (a box is all *Bombus*, then three refined to species), and the Caste column is one click — gyne/worker/drone where `animal_castes` (`schema/119`) says the taxon has castes, female/male elsewhere (the museum databases' words, Peter 2026-10-31; a queen is a gyne). Which taxa have castes is stated, not derived, in `animal_caste` (`schema/045`), nearest stated ancestor winning: the tree has no subgenus to say that cuckoo bumble bees have no workers, and the expert records cannot either (24 *B. flavidus* "workers" on the dev store). Two views over one grid: **By season**, a season in collecting order, chosen from a menu (`sample_season`, `schema/102` — the calendar year of a sample's first day), and **By field number**, the batch: specimens gathered by field number in the order added, kept in `determination_batch` so a box can be worked over several sittings. Numbers are read by `src/determination-numbers.ts` — whole, a unique tail (`412`), or a range whose start may be a tail and whose end may be abbreviated (`9685-697`) — across **every** season, since a box sorted by genus mixes years and numbers before 2025 have seven digits; its problems are codes and the catalog says them, with the span of the person's numbers by length so a dropped digit shows. **Entry writes drafts, never determinations** (Peter, 2026-10-01): `determination_draft` holds one changeable row per specimen and determiner, nothing downstream reads it, and the `commit-determinations` job (1am Pacific, `src/commit-determinations.ts`) turns each into an `in_app` determination in one transaction — before the 4am legacy export, so the export carries the day's work — taking only drafts changed before it began and dropping any that say what the newest volunteer determination already says. That is CONTEXT.md's draft/commit boundary, with one consumer and no per-export bookkeeping; the screen says it as what it means to the person — undo is free until 1am, and after that a change is kept beside the earlier name. A selection that has been named, or had its sex set, from the bar or in one of its rows' own cells, is finished with: the next row ticked or gone to starts a new selection, while unticking only narrows it (`src/app/islands/determine/selection.ts`). Before that a volunteer named one group, selected a second and named it, and the first group took the second name too, most easily through **Go to field number**, which selects the row it finds while the earlier rows are out of sight ([#316](https://github.com/rainhead/beeline/issues/316)). By season, that box stays at the top of the screen with the naming row. A number that is not in the year shown is looked for in every year (`locateSpecimen`, `GET /determinations/locate`), and the page opens that year with the specimen selected, at `/determinations?specimen=<id>`, an address a link to one specimen can use too (beeline-mjml). Moving loads a new page, which clears whatever is ticked, while in the year shown the box adds what it finds to the ticked rows; so with rows ticked it asks first, with a button to open that year, rather than drop them unannounced. Before it moves it waits for every change on the page to save, including any made while it waits, and stays if one failed. Every other way off the page waits the same way (beeline-rt6h): changes are sent one request at a time (`src/app/islands/determine/queue.ts`), so naming rows quickly leaves several waiting, and loading another page cancels the one being sent and never sends the rest. A link or the collection year menu used meanwhile is followed once the changes have been sent, and for what the page cannot hold (closing the tab, Back, a form in the header) the browser asks first. Until 2026-11-17 only the find box waited, and August Jackson reported identifications that were gone after he moved on. Two more faults found then could show a named row without its name, after which a sex chosen on that row was sent without the name and replaced the saved draft. The page's rows are written into the page, so a copy the browser kept and brought back with Back showed them as they were when it loaded; the page now answers `cache-control: no-store`. And By field number, the request that adds or removes numbers is answered with every row, and was sent beside the saves, so it could read a row before a save reached the store and be answered after it; it now takes its turn in the same queue. That replaced asking for an "All years" choice in the menu, which Karla Salp suggested on 2026-11-13 when finding her specimens of one bee across years meant switching the year for each: it would load every specimen a person has into one page, several thousand for her and 40,000 for the busiest collector (Peter, 2026-11-13). The screen calls a season a **collection year**, "By collection year" and "Collection year", since to a volunteer a season reads as spring or summer; the code and CONTEXT.md keep the word season. The name box takes only names from the list, so it turns off the computer's autocorrect and suggested words, which on a Mac replaced half a genus with an English word that matched nothing. Safari also offered the Mac's contacts in the box, whatever `autocomplete` said, so the box's `name` and `id` call it a search, the one thing that stopped that on a test page of variants (beeline-9u06). The checkbox under the list of names, which widens it to wasps and other non-bees, is one setting for the whole page, since the grid makes a new name box for each row it edits. The list closes only when focus leaves the name box, its list and the checkbox together: clicking the checkbox's words moves focus to the checkbox, and the list used to close and take the checkbox with it (beeline-4wx3). Tab from the name box moves to the checkbox and keeps the list open, and ticking it sends focus back to the name box. Ticking the checkbox also searches Ecdysis's list of insect names, for names the tree does not hold yet (beeline-4wx3; Tenthredo, a sawfly, was the first one asked for). The tree's own names go to the browser whole, but Ecdysis's are about a million, so the server searches them (`searchEcdysisNames`, `GET /determinations/ecdysis.json`) in `ecdysis_adoptable` (`schema/025`), a table of the names adoption would accept that is rebuilt whenever the thesaurus is loaded, with each name's family and order worked out once. The first word typed must start the name, so a species is found through its genus (`tenthredo a`), and nothing is asked for under three letters; the tree's matches come first and stay put while Ecdysis answers, so the option Enter takes does not change under the person's finger. Bees are left out, since a bee's name the tree lacks waits for a taxonomist, as it does in the Ecdysis importer: a bee family, or a genus or species filed straight under Apoidea with no family, by one rule (`notABee`, `src/bee-families.ts`) that the search, the draft's check and the commit share. So is anything below a name another accepted name shares at the same rank (the sawfly genus *Euura* is two genera in Ecdysis), because adoption finds a parent by rank and spelling and could hang a species from the wrong one. The table is built walking down from Animalia, once per name; walking up from each name ran out of memory on production's thesaurus. Each name from Ecdysis says "new to Beeline" and shows its family and order, because a spelling can name a katydid as well as a wasp: that is how *Ancistrocercus*, Ben Lee's misspelling of the potter wasp *Ancistrocerus*, came into the tree from Ecdysis as an orthopteran. Picking one adds nothing to the tree. The draft carries the Ecdysis id (`determination_draft.ecdysis_tid`), and the overnight commit adopts the name with whatever ancestors the tree lacks (`src/adopt-ecdysis.ts`) and records the determination against the new node, in the same transaction, so the tree gains a name only when a determination uses it and a name picked by mistake and changed leaves no trace. Adopting when the name was picked was the first plan and was dropped in review: it would have let any volunteer's slip add a genus with its family and order to the tree for good, and adoption's temporary tables cannot share the app's connection. A draft whose name a later fetch of Ecdysis stopped offering waits, and the job's report counts it. For someone working by keyboard or screen reader (beeline-tv0w): the page keeps what it scrolls to clear of the pinned bar, by measuring the bar and setting `scroll-padding-top`, since a browser otherwise brings a focused control only to the top edge of the window, under the bar; closing a row's name box puts focus back on that row's name button; a row's name button, name box and caste buttons each say the row's field number, so rows can be told apart by ear; and what an action did (`Named 5 Osmia.`, `Selected 26000130.`) is written where a screen reader announces it. Reach is the listings' `mine`, following the acting-for switch; the determiner — and owner of drafts and batch — is whoever is signed in, since delegation grants reach and never credit; impersonation looks and cannot write; someone else's specimen answers 404. Expert determinations are deliberately absent from the screen so a volunteer determines rather than copies (beeline-bcq's anti-anchoring note); the listings and the specimen page still show them. `db:reseed` carries none of it and says how much it left behind, like print runs. The design was chosen from three variants on a prototype branch, `prototype/volunteer-determinations`.
