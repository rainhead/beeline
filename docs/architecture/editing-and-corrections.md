# Editing and corrections

Part of Beeline's architecture notes; the index is in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#architecture-overview).

Non-iNat samples are edited in-app (`/samples/:id/edit`, collector-gated): each save updates the live sample row and records correction events keyed by staging Mongo `_id` in `data/corrections.csv` (`src/corrections.ts` — outside the blow-away path), which legacy promotion reads union the git-curated `ingest/legacy-corrections.csv`, app rows winning ([ADR 0004](../adr/0004-correction-overlay.md), beeline-2c3.8). Staff set a sample's **locality** from its page (`/samples/:id`, admin-gated, refused while impersonating; `src/app/locality-override.ts`, beeline-649), because an unprinted iNat-linked sample's locality follows its observation on every promotion and nobody could edit one in the app, so a place name the observation cannot yield had nowhere to go. One form, two durable stores, since the two kinds of sample are named by different things a rebuild reproduces: a sample with an observation goes to `data/sample-overlay.csv` (`src/sample-overlay.ts`, the person overlay's shape plus a `base_value`, keyed `inat:<observation id>` — the link is the sample's identity, and `entity_id` is a per-store draw), applied as the satellite `sample_locality_override` (schema/030) by `src/apply-sample-overlay.ts` at the end of observation promotion and at once from the page; a sample with none goes through the corrections overlay as the collector's own edit would. The follow rule in `ingest/mint-samples.sql` skips overridden samples, `sample_locality_override_stale` asserts the row and the sample agree, and `sample_locality_override_diverged` (schema/175) names an observation that has since moved to a third value, which the page shows beside the override rather than resolving: a deliberate assertion beats an unreviewed upstream edit (ADR 0004). Removing the override hands an unprinted sample back to its observation at once; a printed one keeps what the label says. Promotion reads the file only when given its path, so a scratch promotion never reads the deployed store's decisions; the nightly reports rows that name no sample in the run's detail and goes on. The coordinates take the same form (beeline-942): a second field in the same file, `coordinates`, valued `<latitude> <longitude> [uncertainty m]`, applied as `sample_location_override` with `sample_location` written beside it as `staff_entry`, the provenance every read already carries — and the location upgrade in `ingest/promote-observations.sql`, which used to rewrite `sample_location` unconditionally and so erased a staff point on the next nightly, skips overridden samples. The base it records is the `sample_location` row as it stood, whatever its source, so removal hands an unprinted sample back exactly what it had, an imported point included, and a sample that had none goes back to none; the elevation is left for `sample_elevation_stale` to notice, never cleared by the writer. The observation's believed-true point is now the view `observation_location` (schema/105), which promotion's candidate table reads and the diverged view compares against, with `sample_elevation_stale`'s 5e-5° tolerance. The form appears on the coordinates block even where the sample has none, because that is the case it exists for: an obscured observation with no trusted access yields no coordinates and cannot print, and only a person can put the true point in. Offered only on samples with an observation: the corrections overlay would land an imported sample's point as `legacy_import`, misstating who said so.

Staff **holds** and **waivers** (beeline-plu, beeline-1kb.9; *Hold* and *Waiver* in [CONTEXT.md](../../CONTEXT.md)) are two more fields in the same file, set from the flags card on `/samples/:id` (`src/app/qc-decisions.ts`, admin-gated, refused while impersonating) and applied by the same applier: `hold`, valued `held`, becomes a `sample_hold` row, and `waiver:<rule>`, valued `waived`, becomes a `sample_qc_waiver` row whose `base_value` is what the flag said when it was waived — the thing the waiver is tied to, as a locality override's base is what the observation said (schema/050). A reason is required for both, and the applier refuses a standing one without, rather than inserting a row the CHECK would fail the whole promotion over; it refuses a waiver on an unknown, non-blocking or unwaivable flag the same way, and `sample_qc_finding` ignores one on an unwaivable flag even if it reaches the table. A hold is a finding (`qc_rule_staff_hold`), so printability, the front page and the listings need nothing of their own; a waiver takes its finding out of `sample_qc_finding` while it is in force — the flag still says what it said when waived — which the applier settles each time it runs (`refreshWaivers`, after every promotion and every staff decision) and stores as `in_force`, because comparing the text in the view made every finding build its text and printability 80% slower with no waivers at all. `observation_missing_upstream` says when the observation was last returned rather than counting the runs that missed it, so a waiver on it does not lapse at the next sync. Waivers are also rechecked at the end of every sync and every person-overlay application, since a completed run and a moved iNaturalist account both change what a flag says before any promotion; and `qc_rule_sample_number` joins its two cases into one finding per sample, because a waiver names a flag by its sample and rule. Only samples with an observation take either, since the overlay names a sample by it and every sample still waiting to print has one.
