# Browsing: scope, filters, sort

Part of Beeline's architecture notes; the index is in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#architecture-overview).

Browsing lives at `/samples` and `/specimens` (`src/app/listings.ts` + `src/app/views/listings.tsx`, beeline-2c3.21): the same paged table at two grains, with scope, filters, search, sort and page all in the query string so a filtered listing is a shareable URL, and a CSV of exactly what the filters select — in Darwin Core terms wherever one exists (the pin's number is `fieldNumber` and `catalogNumber` the museum's, filled only where Ecdysis has reported it, per ADR 0008's amendment and gh-98; the sample number is `fieldNumber` on the samples download and `sampleNumber` on the specimens one), UTF-8 with a BOM, `\n` line endings, and streamed whole a page at a time, so it has no cap and no line that is not a record; a numeric cell is never formula-guarded, since a guarded longitude is `'-123.26`, text. Reshaped with Peter and Nora on the sandbox (2026-09-16): each column heading opens a menu holding that column's two sort orders and its filter — a listing *describes* its columns as `TableColumn` specs and `DataTable` draws them, through the one `ColumnMenu` in the component library (the header's `<details class="menu">` with the panel fixed so the wrapper's sideways scroll cannot clip it), so every menu wears the same chevron, the order in force is a separate arrow with `aria-sort` on the heading cell, and no screen can draw a heading differently from the next; it was written out twice at first, for the listings and for People, and the two had drifted within a day (Peter, 2026-09-17) — whose form carries the rest of the query as hidden inputs (`listingParams` is the one place the query becomes a query string, so hidden inputs and hrefs agree); what is in force reads off a row of dismissable pills, not off ten controls. Above the table sit the search box and a three-way toggle — my records, my atlas's (from `person_membership`), all — with other atlases and the ground outside them reached through the Atlas column and shown as a pill. There is no season filter: the dashboard's link to settled seasons names a date, the day before `season.active_since`. A record's number is a pill, the iNaturalist link is the wordmark, the host plant is a column on both listings, the samples listing says whether each sample was netted or trapped — a Method column, the sample page's word, with a filter and no order, since sorting two values only puts one first; the filter (`kind`) narrows specimens too, by the sample they came from — the specimens listing also carries the date the determination of record was made — an order and no filter, since "what came back from the taxonomist this winter" is asked by sorting, and a dash where the source gave no date, which is most legacy determinations — and on a wide screen the heading row is sticky — which needs the wrapper to stop being a scroll container, since sticky positions against the nearest one, so `main > .table-scroll` is `overflow: visible` from 64rem up. A session is keyed by `inat_user_id`, never by `person.entity_id`: that is a per-store sequence draw a rebuild or `db:reseed` redraws, so a session holding one resolved to whoever inherited the number and a volunteer browsed as somebody else under the `mine` scope forced for them (beeline-ten). The person is resolved through `inat_account` per request, which also makes unbinding an account end its sessions and rebinding move them. A private store predating that change is repaired at boot — it outlives the blow-away era, so its schema changes are patched in `src/app/db.ts` rather than by rebuild, per table rather than all-or-nothing, because a store that holds some of its tables and not others is a state that patch itself can create. Resolution failing is not revocation: an unbound session stops sliding its `last_seen_at` so the idle cutoff never reaches it, and rebinding that iNat user would revive every cookie ever issued for it as whoever now holds the account — so `endSessionsFor` runs on both sides of an account change and the idle purge collects orphans. Scope is `mine` (where everyone starts), one atlas, `outside` (collected where no member atlas reaches), or all of them, and every scope is open to everyone: a volunteer sees every record, at minimum what iNaturalist already shows them (Peter, 2026-10-23, beeline-20w3). Until then `mine` was forced for volunteers at parse time, and the scope picker, the collector filter and the `member` filter were staff's. Scope asks where a sample fell; the `member` filter asks where its collector belongs, and the two genuinely disagree — most records from outside the atlases are members travelling (beeline-lcl). **What a reader may not see is a location**: where a sample's observation is obscured or private on iNaturalist (`sample.geoprivacy` or `taxon_geoprivacy`), or the sample cites an observation no sync has read, so that nobody here knows whether it is (1,537 legacy-linked samples on the dev store until the nightly began reading them by id, and the 404 whose observation iNaturalist no longer has after), and the reader is neither staff nor one of its collectors — a co-collector counts, and so does whoever acts for one, since a delegate reads a household's records as the household does — the row carries no coordinates, uncertainty, elevation or locality, and no county either where the observation is private, which is what iNaturalist shows them. iNaturalist's own shifted point is not offered instead; nobody has a use for it (Peter). The rule is `withheld` in `src/app/listings.ts`, applied in the query rather than the view — the location join yields nothing and the place columns read through a CASE — so the place filter, the place sort and the CSV all see the hidden values, and a search cannot find a record by a locality it would not show; the record pages reuse it, and drop with it the observation's place guess, the overrides, the history of where the sample was and what its findings quote. The observer's notes stay, as iNaturalist shows them to everyone, though they are where a volunteer writes the spot: iNaturalist's own handling is half-hearted and nobody has a better rule (Peter, 2026-10-23). Downloads carry coordinates otherwise, with their provenance and geoprivacy beside them: the open per-atlas question ([docs/questions.md](../questions.md)) is about revealing taxon-obscured coordinates *downstream* — on labels, to Ecdysis and GBIF — not about showing a participant their own data, and CONTEXT.md's stance is that anyone trusted with this store is trusted with them. Dashboards scope to the active seasons: the current one, and last season until 1 March, when it settles (`season`/`settled_sample`, CONTEXT.md's **Season**), still flagged and still fixable but no longer asking.

Bulk actions on `/specimens` are chosen first: a button names the action, and pressing it puts the listing in that action's mode (`act` in the query, carried by `listingHref` like any filter, and kept by Clear), with tick boxes only on the rows it can take (beeline-1kb.23). Marking labels for reprint is the only one so far; [Printing](printing.md) describes it.
