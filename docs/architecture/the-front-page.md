# The front page

Part of Beeline's architecture notes; the index is in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#architecture-overview).

`/` is one table (`src/app/dashboard.ts` + `src/app/views/qc.tsx`, reshaped with Peter and Nora on the sandbox, 2026-09-16): the person's samples that want something — a flag to fix, labels still to print — and the observations they numbered but left at 0 specimens, which minting refuses (`observation_sample_candidate`) and which are on the page for exactly that reason: a placeholder made in the field is what a volunteer forgets to fill in. Beside those are the observations in the project with their sample number left blank (`observation_unnumbered`, `schema/109`, beeline-a04): the three synced projects require `sampleId` to join and accept it empty, so `observation_field.sample_number_field_attached` keeps "attached but blank" apart from "absent", which `sample_number_raw` cannot. Their notes are quoted back, since some volunteers wrote the number there, and are never parsed for one: '1C red cuckoo' is why. Below the table, advisory, is the list of days whose run of sample numbers skips one (`sample_number_gap`, beeline-virz), each linked to everything the volunteer observed on iNaturalist that day, in the project or not, and wherever it was — the link says `place_id=any`, since iNaturalist otherwise narrows the list to the viewer's home place. Numbers restart every collecting day, so a gap is judged per day, and only for collectors who number that way. A jump of more than three starts a second run (`1, 2, 3, 101, 102`) rather than counting as missed samples. A day whose unnumbered observations account for its missing numbers is left out. A flag names the column it is about — sample, place, coordinates, host plant, specimens — and that cell is marked, with the flag's words on a full-width line under the row; `DASHBOARD_RULES` is the list of rules a volunteer sees here and the column each maps to, and a rule absent from it is not this page's business (a count that fell after printing means labels to discard, not a job; beeline-69t reviews the rest). The page never says "sync": it states the schedule — every morning at 2am Pacific — and not the last run's timestamp, which the sandbox printed in UTC as 9am and which is `/jobs`'s concern. Below it is **Check iNaturalist now** (`src/app/inat-check.ts`, beeline-yso), so a volunteer who has just fixed an observation sees the flag clear at once: it reads every observation the person made this season from each synced project, one project at a time because trust is per project, whatever iNaturalist says changed (Peter mistrusts its `updated_at`, 2026-10-16), then runs the nightly's own promotion on `mintConn` behind the lock the unknown collectors' screen uses (`src/app/promotion-lock.ts`), with the app's own token, since volunteers' were dropped. Once a minute per observer, counted from the attempt; refused while the nightly or the Sunday sweep is reading iNaturalist, since both rewrite `observation_field` whole; acting for someone checks theirs, and impersonation sees the button and is refused. Each run records whose observations it read in `sync_run.observer_user_id`, which keeps it from moving the nightly's watermark (`lastSyncStart`) and lets deletion detection count it as covering that observer only. Above the table, a note says how many of the open season's specimens nobody has named, linking to `/determinations` on that season; it counts what that screen calls "to name" (`entrySeasons`), against the signed-in determiner's drafts even while acting for someone, and is absent at zero. The specimen page points to the same screen when the viewer's own specimen has no volunteer determination. Settled seasons stay off it and are counted (beeline-2c3.24); a settled sample stays only while a run already holds its labels, until they are mailed, since a season's end does not cancel a print job already under way. The heading is the brand with a lede for a new volunteer, not a worklist title; the summary — "2 samples need attention" — is the second heading, since it is what the volunteer came for, and the schedule note sits under the table. Where the person belongs to an atlas (`person_membership`, never where their samples fell), the header ends in that atlas's mark, as it does on their `/people` page; the marks and colorways live in one table, `ATLAS_IDENTITY` (`src/app/theme/atlas.ts`), and are for now the 216px JPEGs the program distributes on Canvas, cropped to their circle and drawn small until the vector artwork arrives (beeline-2c3.13).
